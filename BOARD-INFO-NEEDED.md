# Website: what we need from the board

The American Friends for Ukraine website is built and working as a preview. It has the donation pop-up, the projects, the FAQ, and a gallery of real photos from Ukraine. What's left is mostly information only the board can give, plus a few decisions.

Each item says what we need and where it shows up on the site. **Part 1 blocks launch.** Parts 2 and 3 can follow soon after. Please reply with answers, files or "skip" for each item, and tick the boxes as you go.

---

## Part 1: Needed before we can launch

### Legal and tax

- [ ] **Exact legal name** as registered with the state and IRS (for example, "American Friends for Ukraine, Inc."). *Shows in the footer and on donation receipts.*
- [ ] **EIN** (federal tax ID, XX-XXXXXXX). *Shows in the donate section and footer. Donors and employer-matching programs ask for it.*
- [ ] **501(c)(3) status:** approved, or still pending? If approved, the date on the IRS letter. *Changes the tax wording on the site.*
- [ ] **Tax-deductible wording, checked by your accountant.** The site currently says gifts are "tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law." If status is still pending, tell us what we're allowed to say.

### Donations

- [ ] **Which donation platform** we'll use (Zeffy, Givebutter, Donorbox, PayPal Giving Fund, or another). *Every "Donate" button connects to it.* Zeffy is free for nonprofits. Setting one up needs the EIN and the organization's bank account.
- [ ] **The donation link or "embed code"** from that platform, once the account is set up.
- [ ] **Monthly giving:** yes or no? If yes, the monthly-gift link (usually from the same platform).
- [ ] **Mailing address for checks**, and who to make checks payable to.
- [ ] **What a gift buys.** The donate pop-up shows examples like "$100: a trauma first-aid kit." The current ones are made up. Please give 5 real examples for $25, $50, $100, $250 and $500, or tell us to remove them.

### Contact

- [ ] **Public contact email**, ideally on our own domain (for example, info@americanfriendsforukraine.org), and **who will answer it**.

### Who we are

- [ ] **Mission statement, approved by the board.** Current draft:
  > American Friends for Ukraine connects American generosity with proven humanitarian work in Ukraine. We raise tax-deductible donations in the U.S., vet the projects we support, and report back to donors on how every dollar is used.
- [ ] **Board members:** name and role for each person, and confirmation that each one is OK being listed publicly.

### Our partner in Ukraine

- [ ] **Partner organization's name**, as they'd like it written.
- [ ] **2–4 sentences about them:** who they are, how long they've worked, and what they do.
- [ ] **Their website**, if they have one.
- [ ] **Their permission** to be named on our site (and to use their logo, if they'd like it shown).

### Projects

The site lists the **Medical Supply Caravan** and space for one more project.

- [ ] **Caravan fundraising goal** (for example, $25,000).
- [ ] **Caravan dates and route:** where it starts, and which towns or clinics it reaches.
- [ ] **Caravan status:** upcoming, active or completed.
- [ ] **A second project** (name, 1–2 sentences, goal), or tell us to remove that card.

### Promises the site makes

The site makes these promises to donors. Please confirm the board is comfortable keeping each one, or tell us what to change:

- [ ] "**Board-reviewed projects**": the board reviews each project's plan and budget before funding it.
- [ ] "**Updates for every donor**": donors will hear back with updates, photos and reports. *How often, and who sends them?*
- [ ] "**We report back to donors on how every dollar is used.**"
- [ ] **How much of each gift goes to the work** (for example, "We are volunteer-run, and 95% of donations go directly to projects"). This is one of the questions donors ask most. If you don't know yet, we'll leave it out.

### Photos

The site now uses real photos from Ukraine instead of illustrations. Before they go public:

- [ ] **Permission from the people in the photos.** Several volunteers' faces are clearly visible, including the volunteer carrying boxes at the top of the page and the volunteer with the flag. Please confirm each person is OK with being on a public website.
- [ ] **Safety check.** Do any photos show something that could put people at risk: a recognizable location, a vehicle license plate (two truck photos show plates), or someone who shouldn't be identified? We can blur details or remove photos.
- [ ] **Who took the photos?** The site says "taken by [our volunteers / partners]". Which is right? Should we credit anyone by name?
- [ ] **Captions:** we wrote them from what each photo shows. If you know the place or date of any photo (for example, "Kyiv, May 2025"), send it. Captions with real places and dates are more convincing.
- [ ] **Sensitive photos:** the gallery includes photos of war damage, including blast damage at a mosque. Please confirm you're comfortable showing these. We left out a memorial with children's toys and a photo of people praying.

### Web address

- [ ] **Domain name** (for example, americanfriendsforukraine.org). Has one been bought? If not, who will buy it? About $10–20/year.
- [ ] **Who owns the accounts.** The domain, hosting and donation accounts should be in the organization's name and shared by at least two board members, not tied to one person's personal account.

## Part 3: Decisions for the board

Quick yes/no answers are fine.

- [ ] **Email updates:** add a "Get updates" sign-up to the site? (It needs someone to send updates, and a free tool like Mailchimp or Buttondown.)
